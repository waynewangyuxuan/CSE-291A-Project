The other 457 episodes have no judgment in this semantic review; absence of a rule match is not evidence of ignoring injection.

This is unblinded agent review after observing the cohort. The labels describe action alignment and quotation, and do not establish hidden intent, a full authorization judgment, or an independently validated judge.
